# Spreads on Toast Brand

## Brand idea

**Call your shot. Live with the crumbs.**

Spreads on Toast is the independent baseball prediction game for people who believe the best part
of a season is having an opinion before anyone knows the answer. It turns preseason win-total calls
into a daily ritual: check the slate, watch the line, move up the table, and remind your friends
what you knew in March.

The name is the memorable hook, not a mandate for food-themed decoration. Toast appears as a quiet
signature—warm color, crisp edges, “toasted” outcomes, and the occasional crumb of copy—inside a
product that otherwise takes baseball seriously.

## Personality

- **Confident, not corporate.** It knows the game and gets to the point.
- **Competitive, not hostile.** Bragging rights are the prize; friends remain friends.
- **Editorial, not administrative.** Every screen has hierarchy and a point of view.
- **Warm, not cute.** Toast is wit and texture, never novelty clip art.
- **Alive, not noisy.** Scores and movement create energy; chrome stays calm.

## Voice and tone

Write like a sharp friend in the group chat who also keeps the scorebook.

- Prefer “Your card is locked” to “The submission has been finalized.”
- Prefer “3 picks left” to “Completion: 90%.”
- Prefer “First pitch in 42m” to a raw timestamp.
- Prefer “Called it” and “Got toasted” for celebratory result labels, while preserving plain
  language anywhere clarity or accessibility requires it.
- Use sentence case. Avoid exclamation marks unless something genuinely exceptional happened.
- Never imply real-money wagering. “Line” means the reference win total; competition is for
  bragging rights.

## Brand mark

The mark combines a toast silhouette, home plate, and baseball seams. It must remain readable at
16px, work as a monochrome mask, and avoid fine illustrative detail. The primary lockup uses the
mark plus “Spreads on Toast” in the display face. The short product name is “Spreads,” never “SOT.”

## Visual world

The visual system pairs **ballpark-at-night navy** with **scorebook cream**, **hot-stove red**, and
**toast gold**. Fine ruled lines recall a scorebook. Large condensed headlines and tabular figures
feel like scoreboards. Surfaces are crisp and layered, with restrained rounding and no generic
gradient-glass aesthetic.

Photography is optional. When used, favor human baseball moments—chalk, lights, scorecards,
bleachers, hands—not licensed team imagery as decoration.

## Brand promises

1. You can understand what matters in one glance.
2. Your picks always feel consequential and safely saved.
3. Live baseball feels live.
4. The product is delightful without getting between you and the game.
